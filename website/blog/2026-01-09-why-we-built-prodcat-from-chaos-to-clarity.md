Maintaining product documentation was eating our time and sanity. We built Prodcat to fix that — and here’s how it changed everything

<!--truncate-->

Before Prodcat, we were juggling dozens of Markdown files, manually updating product specs, and constantly breaking navigation. Every new product meant copy-pasting templates, fixing links, and hoping we didn’t miss a field. It was error-prone, slow, and deeply frustrating.

We asked ourselves: _What if documentation could be generated from a single source of truth?_

That’s how Prodcat was born — a tool that reads a JavaScript product list and generates a full Docusaurus site. No more Markdown. No more broken links. Just clean, consistent documentation.

Prodcat isn’t just a generator. It’s a philosophy:

- Centralize your data
- Automate the boring parts
- Focus on what matters — your products

Whether you’re documenting hardware, software, or services, Prodcat helps you scale without losing clarity.
